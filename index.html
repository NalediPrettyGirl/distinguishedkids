About Our Academy

Distinguished Kids Academy is a warm, caring preschool that provides a clean, safe, and friendly environment where children feel secure and happy. We admit children from 6 months to 6 years, offering quality care and early learning that supports their growth and development. Our focus is on nurturing confident, curious, and capable children as we help shape smart, strong future leaders.

Clean Atmosphere

Our classrooms and playgrounds are maintained to the highest standards, offering a warm and welcoming environment for both children and parents.

Your Gateway to Learning

Join Distinguished Kids Academy, simply visit us by appointment and be ready for a whole lot more!

Enrolment Process:
Should you wish to enroll your child we ask that you fill in all the necessary enrolment forms and return via email.
We will contact you to confirm the enrolment and placing of your child.
Should you wish to accept, we ask that the enrolment fee is paid to secure your placement.
We will then require you to complete and sign all required forms.
